# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Small to mid-size HVAC and trades companies. Two audiences share the product:
- **Office / dispatch**: dispatchers and office leads running the schedule board, quotes, invoicing and client follow-ups all day at a desk.
- **Field technicians**: working visits from a phone in a truck, a basement or a rooftop, often gloved, in poor light, between tasks.

The public landing page (`/`) speaks to owners/GMs who buy the software and dispatchers who influence the choice, equally.

## Product Purpose
TradeWerks runs the office and the truck from one record: request → quote → job → visit → invoice. Dispatch books the visit, the tech works it from their phone, status lands on the board live. An assistant does busywork but stops for a human before anything changes. Success is nothing retyped, nothing lost between office and field, and follow-ups that stop when the client answers.

## Brand Personality
Direct, capable, grounded. Plain talk from people who know the trades. Concrete claims over adjectives; show the real workflow rather than describe it. No hype, no startup gloss.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing templates: blue/purple gradients, hero-metric stat bands, identical icon-card grids, glassmorphism.
- "AI-powered" marketing voice that overpromises autonomy (the assistant asks first; the design must never imply otherwise).
- Consumer-app playfulness that undermines trust with owners handling money and schedules.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the real workflow.** Demonstrate the product doing the job (live demos, real records) instead of asserting benefits.
2. **One record, no retyping.** The interface should make continuity across request → invoice visible.
3. **Human in the loop.** Anything automated shows that a person approves it.
4. **Built for the worst conditions.** Field UI assumes gloves, glare and interruption; office UI assumes density and speed.
5. **Plain over clever.** Copy and visuals choose clarity over flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA across all surfaces. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for every animation, including auto-playing demos. Don't rely on color alone for status (field users include color-blind techs; outdoor glare flattens color). Touch targets ≥ 44px on field and mobile surfaces.
